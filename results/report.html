Methodology and interpretation

  • Main formation sample: NYSE/AMEX/Nasdaq common shares (CRSP share codes 10/11), positive market equity, nonfinancial firms (exclude SIC 6000–6999), positive book equity/assets, available earnings, and 24–60 prior monthly return observations. Historical Compustat SIC is used when available, otherwise dated CRSP SIC.
  • Fiscal year-ends in calendar year t−1 explain returns from July t through June t+1. Size is June market equity; valuation denominators use December t−1 market equity. Both market equity and accounts are in USD millions.
  • Book equity = CEQ + TXDB. The primary TXDB specification follows the paper’s common-equity-plus-balance-sheet-deferred-taxes definition. Earnings = IB + TXDI − DVP. Missing tax/dividend add-ons are zero; missing base fields remain missing. Latest fiscal year-end per calendar year is used; domestic USD Compustat records only.
  • CCM LC/LU/LS links with primary status P/C must be valid at formation. Overlaps prefer P, then LC/LU/LS and the latest link start. No extra two-year Compustat seasoning rule is imposed.
  • NYSE size cutoffs use all NYSE common stocks with size data, including financial firms. Size–beta sorts use NYSE beta breakpoints within size; size–B/M sorts use all eligible stocks within size. One-dimensional B/M and E/P cutoffs use the full eligible sample, as in the paper.
  • Pre-ranking beta is the sum of current and one-lag market slopes in a raw-return regression with an intercept. The lag is calendar-aligned before taking the five-year window. Post-ranking betas use full-sample portfolio returns and are assigned to constituent stocks. This intentional ex-post estimation matches the paper; these are not out-of-sample trading signals.
  • Portfolio returns are equal-weighted across observed surviving constituents each month. No future-return availability is required at formation and missing returns are not filled. The legacy CRSP RET series is used without an additional DLRET adjustment; this assumption may affect differences from the original vintage.
  • Monthly cross-sectional OLS uses percentage stock returns and an intercept. Table III ratios are clipped at monthly 0.5/99.5 percentiles before logs. FM t-statistics use the conventional time-series standard error of monthly slopes; no Newey–West adjustment is added.
  • The appendix forms portfolios at December year-end using NYSE stocks and the CRSP NYSE index (MSIA). Financial stocks are included in the appendix, whose tests do not require accounts.
  • Only the descriptive NYSE market rows in Tables VI/AIV use 100 log(1+R). This reporting convention is inferred from the published means/std/t; 1941–1990 NYSE VW has a log mean of 0.931% versus the paper’s 0.93%, while the arithmetic mean is 1.022%. Separate arithmetic diagnostic CSVs are retained. Market betas and all individual-stock regressions still use raw simple returns.
